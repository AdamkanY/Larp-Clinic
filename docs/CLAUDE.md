# docs/

Documents for the grading, the submission and the team: not code, and not read by the app. Keep every number in
them in step with `models/gbm_metrics.json` and the README; if the model is retrained, update them.

| File | Contains | Produced by | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `AI_USE.md` | The record of AI use the course requires: tools and models, the share of AI-written lines per file (from `tools/ai_provenance.py`), the prompts (standing `CLAUDE.md` instructions, task prompts, the 2026-11-10 session), how the output was checked, the reflection (what worked, what not, how we adapted, conclusions) and the list of aids. Parts marked **[team]** are filled in by the team | Claude (draft), team | video slide, Q&A, graders |
| `CONTRIBUTIONS.md` | The contribution matrix: how and why the work was organised, levels (lead, contributor, support), one row per area and one column per member, what git shows, one or two sentences per person. Cells marked **?** are filled in by the team | Claude (draft), team | video slide, Q&A, graders |
| `VIDEO_SCRIPT.md` | Timed script for the 4-minute video (who speaks, what is on screen, what is said), the slides to prepare and a checklist before upload | Claude (draft), Raphael | video |
| `QA_PREP.md` | Likely Q&A questions with short answers and the numbers behind them, grouped by who leads the answer | Claude (draft), team | Q&A on Dec 11 |
| `SUBMISSION.md` | Dates, what to upload to Canvas and how to build the zip, how to test it, and the content checklist | Claude (draft), team | whoever uploads |
